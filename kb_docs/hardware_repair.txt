Hardware Repair and Servicing Guide

Screen Replacements:
If your device has a cracked or unresponsive display, we offer certified repairs. Screen replacements cost 500rs and take 2 business days to complete. Please ensure all data is backed up before handing over your device.

Battery Replacements:
Devices experiencing rapid battery drain or unexpected shutdowns may need a new battery. Battery replacements cost 300rs and are usually completed within 1 business day. 

Water Damage:
Water-damaged devices require a comprehensive diagnostic test, which carries a non-refundable fee of 150rs. Repair costs will be estimated after the diagnostic is complete. Turnaround time for water damage is typically 5 to 7 business days.

Warranty on Repairs:
All hardware repairs come with a 90-day limited warranty covering the replaced parts and labor. This warranty is voided if the device suffers subsequent physical or liquid damage.
