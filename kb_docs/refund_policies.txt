Customer Refund and Return Policy

Standard Return Window:
Customers may return most new, unopened items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging with all accessories included.

Processing Time:
Once we receive your returned item, our team will inspect it within 2 business days. If approved, the refund will be initiated immediately. It typically takes 3 to 5 business days for the funds to appear on your original payment method, depending on your bank.

Non-Refundable Items:
The following items are strictly non-refundable:
Downloadable software products
Gift cards
Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance"
Devices with physical damage caused by the user

Return Shipping:
If the return is a result of our error (you received an incorrect or defective item), we will pay the return shipping costs. In all other cases, the customer is responsible for return shipping fees.
